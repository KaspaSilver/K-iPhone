# KaChat audit, 2026-11-20

**Code:** commit d7c56280 plus the uncommitted work since it: the kUSDT port, Send Dollars and
`token_payment_notice`, the Wallet / Profile split, Connection Settings cards and Address Visibility.
**Method:** read-only. Five reviewers covered money and kUSDT, messaging and remote input,
on-device security, smoothness, and dead code. I re-checked the items marked ✔ in the code myself.
**Not covered:** App Store review items, by standing request.

**Result:**
- No funds can be lost, and keys, seed handling, auth gates, NFC and the App Group all held up.
- One High in new code: a contact can make a **verified** fake "Received kUSDT" bubble.
- One High in older code: an animated image any group member sends can crash the app every time
  the chat opens.
- kUSDT refreshes are fragile: a tab switch cancels them, and the 90-second poll asks Trestly about
  every address of every user.
- Little dead Swift. The big cleanups are the notification extension's stale strings (about 5,500
  lines) and duplicated address screens.

## Status after fixes (2026-11-20)

Every security, privacy and smoothness item below is fixed in the working tree (not built, not
committed), plus dead-code item 24 (Coin Control labels). Except:

- **Left as is, with reasons:**
  - **Seed readable while locked** (Keychain `AfterFirstUnlock`). Background sync and the
    notification extension derive spending addresses from it; tightening it needs a migration
    and a check of every background path, so it's a separate change.
  - **Kaspa Discover's first pull derives up to 1,000 addresses on the main thread.** That sweep's
    account scoping (IOS-107) makes moving it off main a larger change. Later pulls use the cache.
  - **The kUSDT txid isn't recomputed locally.** The submitted transaction is now checked
    output by output, input by input and by fee; a mismatching id from the node is logged.
- **Behaviour changes to know:**
  - An unverified kUSDT payment bubble shows "Unverified kUSDT payment" with no amount until the
    chain confirms it.
  - The regular kUSDT look (every 90 s) asks Trestly only about the Chatting Address, addresses
    with kUSDT or kUSDT history, the newest three revealed and any a receive screen showed in the
    last week. Manage kUSDT, Discover, Send Dollars and the Wallet pull still look at every address.
  - Pulling Wallet ends the spinner once balances are read; "Scanning your addresses for
    balances…" shows under the card while the sweeps finish.
  - Only GIFs up to 1,024 px and 300 frames animate; animated WebP / PNG / HEICS show still.
    Images over 50 MP aren't shown (placeholder).
  - An invalid reaction emoji reads as a removal of that sender's reaction.
  - A kUSDT review rebuilt at the swipe that now costs more, or comes from another address, stops
    with "This send changed while the screen was open…" instead of sending.
  - The kUSDT builder's web view is blocked from the network by a content rule list, restarts
    after a crash or a 20 s stall, and is closed a minute after its last use.
  - Delete Account also clears profile image caches and the URL cache for every account (they
    load again).
- **Also fixed, from KaPay's audit of the shared kUSDT code:**
  - **Paying twice after "Not confirmed":** the send no longer drops its coins; the next send
    from them picks a "Not confirmed" send's coin first (`ChatService.unknownOutcomeKeys`, set by
    `submitRpcTransaction`), so the two conflict and at most one lands.
  - **Spent coins counted again:** coins a send is holding are left out of the balances and of
    every coin pick (`ChatService.heldOutpointKeys`).
  - **A coin listed twice by Trestly** counts once.
  - **Discover** reveals exactly the slot it found and re-checks the account after that.
  - **Trestly lookups** use their own cookie-less, cache-less session.
- **Also fixed, from KaPost's audit of the same code:** a kUSDT review could send a build made
  for an earlier From, To or amount (a build still running when you went Back and changed it).
  A build is now used only when it matches what the review shows, and the builder's numbers are
  read without overflow traps.
- **Also fixed beyond the list:** group photos and avatar / link-preview thumbnails were decoded
  at full size too (same crash); the admin can now remove a group photo that won't decode.
- **Not done (dead code, your call):** items 22, 23, 25-28.
- **Found while fixing, not changed:** `KNSAvatarView.swift:67` (contact backup photos) and
  `NextcloudPickerView.swift:256`, `:266` still decode images at full size.

## Checked and fine

- **Kaspa sends:** owner check, other-network refusal, fee-vs-Review check, no Retry after an unknown outcome.
- **kUSDT:** coins are believed only at the recomputed script with kUSDT's covenant id on the node; amounts are locale-safe and overflow-checked; script, quantum and wrong-network recipients are refused; submit holds outpoints and Try Again resends the same transaction. The three shipped kUSDT files match their pins.
- **Messaging:** the 5.3 voice-duration clamp holds; group roster, signature and replay checks hold; deleted messages stay deleted on the 1:1, archive and group paths; the push sender is proved from input signatures.
- **Device:** Keychain items are device-only and Secure Enclave wrapped; `DeviceAuth` refuses without a passcode; seed and key copies expire in 30 s; NFC uses PBKDF2 600k with AES-GCM; the App Group holds no keys or plaintext; no ATS exceptions and no `http://`; no analytics or crash SDKs.
