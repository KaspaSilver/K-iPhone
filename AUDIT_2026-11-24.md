# K audit, 2026-11-24

**Code:** commit 7e2e0d54 plus the uncommitted work of 2026-11-23/24: Swap (ChangeNOW and kUSDT
through Trestly), .kachat only, the KaPlay arcade (Snake, 2048), the Chess and profile cycle
buttons, Tip in chats, Who Can Message You?, the composer previews, the quick composer over games,
and the scroll-smoothness changes.
**Method:** read-only. Six reviewers:
1. keys and money;
2. messaging and push;
3. untrusted input and privacy;
4. KaPost and KaPlay;
5. smoothness and battery;
6. UI, strings and dead code.

Items marked ✔ I re-checked in the code myself.
**Not covered:** App Store review items, by standing request. Nothing that AUDIT_2026-11-20.md,
AUDIT_2026-11-21.md or AUDIT_5.3.md lists as fixed or "left as is" is repeated. Owner decisions
(no Face ID on sends, no App Switcher cover, .kachat prices to miners) are not findings.

**Result:**
- No Critical issues. One High, in the new Swap: XRP deposits can get stuck.
- Most of the rest are in work from the last two days:
  - Swap needs another pass: the deposit card, comma decimals, per-account state, the XRP tag.
  - Share to KaPost from a game can post twice.
  - The arcade can drop a score that did land.
  - Some of today's scrolling memos are keyed too loosely.
- One older regression: removing KNS also removed the recovery for unfinished KNS reveals.
- About 95 texts on screen have no translation key, so they show in English in every language.
  Every key that exists is in all 19 languages.

**Status, 2026-11-24: everything fixed in code, built, not yet committed.** Partial or
different from the suggested fix:

  but it is not tested against the live API yet; if every ChangeNOW swap is refused, look here
  first.
- #9: a stamp the REST API can't answer for stays on the board, marked unconfirmed, and is
  re-checked about once a minute while the boards refresh. It goes for good only on a definite
  "not found" more than 10 minutes after sending.
- #11: a running bot game holds challenges for the bot screen's whole life, so a finished win's
  stamp can still go out.
- #13: both sides. The extension files the request senders it silenced; the app rings each one
  once when it learns their name.
- #24: replaced by a one-pass `Layout` (`KaPostActionRowLayout`) rather than a tighter guess.

**Checked and fine** (highlights from the six reviews):
- **Swap's kUSDT path:**
  - The quote is cleared on every edit, flip and From change.
  - The 1% minimum can't underflow.
  - Nothing is signed before Approve, and the key is wiped in a `defer`.
  - Maybe-sent trades hold the address.
- **Chat Tip:** the amount is fixed when the card appears, the caps hold, and an unknown outcome removes the card.
- **Secrets:** seed and key copies are `localOnly` with a 30 s expiry, and the seed screens react to screen capture.
- **The arcade engines match ARCADE.md exactly.** The test vectors were re-run with a separate port.
- **2048's sliding tiles always agree with the engine:** it falls back to the engine on any mismatch, and nothing can go out of range.
- **Challenge links** rebuild their URLs from checked fields only, and their matchers run in linear time.
- **The composer's link previews** apply the feed's rules. Page, redirect and image allow-lists hold on every hop.
- **Web views** (Trestly relay, in-app browser) are https-only and allow-listed, with non-persistent stores.
- **Node peers:** private, loopback and CGNAT addresses are dropped.
- **Today's scrolling changes are correct** (no regressions beyond #10, #17, #24 and #30):
  - `activeNetwork` invalidates with settings, and the identity fast path matches the old check.
  - The day ranges are right on DST days.
  - The GIF buffer check falls back to a full compare.
  - The chat list's unchanged-write skip hides nothing a row shows.
- **Background:** polls are gated on app state, and chess screens pair acquire and release.
